Battery on the cap

The V1.20 frame has nothing that locates the battery above the step at z −15.2, and nothing stops it lifting into the roof. This redesign carries the battery in a printed cradle on the cap, closed by a screwed bar, so cap and battery come off together. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

New part · pins 4–5

Retainer bar, 2 × M2 × 6

A 1.5 mm bar across the battery's front face screws into two bosses at the front ends of the cradle walls. It closes the cradle into a ring, so the battery is fully captured even with the cap in your hand. It sits below plug 1 and clears the switch body by 0.3 mm.

Wiring and charging

  1. Switch up through the floor panel and the DFR1202 clipped in, exactly as V1.20. Plug 2 parks in the front bay beside the board.
  2. Cut lead S's battery end to 75–80 mm and leave it looped in the robot-right side space, below plug 1.
  3. Battery into the cradle from the front, leads up through the gaps between the roof tabs, then the retainer bar and its two M2 × 6 screws.
  4. Plug the battery into plug 1, tuck the loop, offer the cap up and fit the three cap screws.
  5. Charging: three cap screws out, pull the cap back about 40 mm, unplug plug 1 beside the box. The battery stays captured in the cap; two bar screws release it.
